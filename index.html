Come for slow mornings, local walks, bonfires, mountain views and a side of Himachal that feels far away from the usual tourist circuit.

The route

Four days.
One slow journey.

Aut → Upper Raila → Upper Neahi → Shangarh → Aut. The itinerary mixes village life, heritage, forest walks, waterfalls, meadows and mountain drives.

DAY 01 · RAILA

Village & heritage

Check in, lunch, guided village walk and the Twin Towers / Dhalyara Kothi.

DAY 02 · BHATKANDHA

Golden hour

Optional sunrise walk, local life and a 4×4 ride to Bhatkandha Meadow.

DAY 03 · NEAHI

Waterfall & lake

Rupirella Waterfall, Sainj Fernway, government school and Pundrikrishi Lake.

DAY 04 · SHANGARH

Meadows & homeward

4×4 to Shangarh, village and waterfall, café lunch and drop to Aut.

Day by day

The itinerary.

Day 01
Aut → Upper RailaVillage Hike & Twin Towers of Raila
  • Pick-up from Aut and transfer to The Rabbit House.
  • Check-in, lunch and an afternoon guided village hike.
  • Visit the Twin Towers / Dhalyara Kothi and explore traditional architecture.
  • Dinner, bonfire and overnight at The Rabbit House.
Day 02
Raila → BhatkandhaVillage Life & Golden Hour
  • Optional sunrise walk with the homestay's three friendly Himalayan dogs.
  • Breakfast, guided village hike, farms, forest paths and local life.
  • 4×4 drive to Bhatkandha Sunset Point and walk to the meadow.
  • Sunset, 4×4 return, dinner and overnight.
Day 03
Upper Raila → Upper NeahiWaterfall, School & Lake
  • Scenic mountain drive towards Upper Neahi.
  • En-route stop at Rupirella Waterfall.
  • Check-in at Sainj Fernway, Upper Neahi.
  • Visit the government school and Pundrikrishi Lake.
Day 04
Neahi → Shangarh → AutMeadows, Waterfall & Homeward Drive
  • Breakfast and check-out.
  • 4×4 drive to Shangarh and explore the village and meadow.
  • Visit Shangarh Waterfall and stop for lunch at a local café.
  • Evening Shangarh → Aut drop. Journey ends.
